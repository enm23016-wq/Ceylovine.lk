The Beginning

A Different Way to See Nature.

✳

CEYLOVINE began with a simple question: what new possibilities could emerge from Sri Lanka's underutilized fruits?

Inspired by Lovi, scientifically known as Flacourtia inermis, our entrepreneurship project explores fruit value addition through the development of a distinctive fruit wine concept.

Our journey connects local agricultural resources, product development, research, packaging design and a vision for sustainable entrepreneurship.

Our Journey

From an Idea to a Product Concept.

Every entrepreneurial idea begins with a question. Our journey brings together field observations, research, mentoring, experimentation and packaging design.

✳

This is an evolving project. Each stage helps us understand the opportunities, practical challenges and further testing needed to develop the concept responsibly.

STAGE 01

Identifying the Opportunity

Exploring the potential of Lovi fruit and investigating the challenges associated with underutilized agricultural resources.

STAGE 02

Research & Market Understanding

Gathering insights through surveys, field observations, conversations and relevant literature to understand potential customer preferences and market challenges.

STAGE 03

Technical Learning

Exploring fermentation and beverage development through technical mentoring, process observation and product-development activities.

STAGE 04

Product Evaluation

Developing plans for quality evaluation, laboratory analysis, hygiene controls and appropriate product documentation.

STAGE 05

Packaging & Brand Identity

Creating the CEYLOVINE identity through bottle labels, neck tags, heritage-inspired packaging and a consistent visual language.
